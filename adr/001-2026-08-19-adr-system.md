# ADR 001 — Adopt an ADR system; retire the monolithic plan

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decided:** 2026-08-19
- **Evidence:** this repo's history; `docs/provenance/verify-plan-shards.mjs`

## Context

`IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` had reached **11,459 lines**. It was append-only by constitutional
rule, which is what made it trustworthy and also what made it unusable: a reader looking for
"why is there no vector store" had to know that the answer lives at line 9516, under a heading
called `M2 DECISION MEMO`, and that the option set it decides between was framed 8,900 lines
earlier inside Stage 2.

Three concrete symptoms, all measured rather than felt:

1. **Decisions were not addressable.** The file's 129 headings mix work-tracking tables,
   pre-registrations, results, corrections, and one decision memo. Nothing distinguishes
   "we chose X over Y, and here is the argument" from "task F8 is complete".
2. **Structure had already begun to rot.** Lines 10872–11359 contain `## Design`,
   `## Hypotheses`, `## Gates`, and `## Verdict` headings that lost their parent when
   E1-HOIST was appended at `#` level rather than `###`. Append-only discipline preserves
   content perfectly and structure not at all.
3. **The file was load-bearing for 163 other files.** 663 citations point at it, mostly
   TSDoc in `src/` citing by section name.

## Options considered

| | approach | why not |
|---|---|---|
| A | Freeze the plan in place with a banner | Safest — zero edits elsewhere — but it does not actually break the document up, which was the point |
| B | Archive to `.history/` and rewrite all 663 citations | Follows the existing `.history/NNN-…` convention, but a 163-file sweep for a purely organisational gain |
| **C** | **Shard verbatim; leave a routing stub at the cited path** | **Chosen** |

## Decision

**Decisions move to `adr/`; the record they were drawn from is sharded, not summarised away.**

- `adr/NNN-YYYY-MM-DD-<feature>.md` — one ADR per *decision theme*. `NNN` is a monotone
  counter; the date is the date the **decision** was taken, not the date the file was
  written. Backfilled ADRs (002–013) carry a `Recorded:` line saying so.
- `adr/proposals/<feature>/` — the supporting material: `PLAN-EXCERPT.md` (the verbatim
  shard), `EVAL.md` (which eval scripts and result artifacts are this decision's
  instruments), and any handoffs or reviews.
- `FINDINGS.md` stays at the root, unchanged in role: the single consolidated index of
  settled claims, dead hypotheses, and unread measurements. **It is the one file that is
  edited in place.** ADRs are append-mostly; `FINDINGS.md` is derived.
- `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` remains at its path as a **routing stub** listing every original
  heading, its original line number, and its shard. All 663 citations keep resolving in one
  hop, including the handful that cite by line.

**New work does not append to the plan.** It appends to the relevant ADR, or opens a new one.

### The split had to be provably lossless

Sharding an append-only evidence record is the one operation here that can destroy evidence
silently — and quietly dropping forty lines would look exactly like success. So the shards
were created in their own commit (`69a587e`), by a line-range partition of `[1, 11459]` checked
for gaps and overlaps, with `docs/provenance/verify-plan-shards.mjs` reassembling them in
original line order and asserting **byte-for-byte equality (813,230 bytes)**. That verifier
runs only at `69a587e`, the last commit where the root file was still the full document.

### Eval scripts stay in `eval/`

The original intent was to move each experiment's scripts into its proposal directory. That
was **abandoned on measurement**, not on taste. `eval/` holds 153 relative imports, and the
cross-track ones cut straight through the ADR boundaries: `scale-rank-check.mjs` and
`scale-score.mjs` (ADR 002) are imported by `declex-*` and `idfuse-*` (ADR 004);
`e1-phase-schedule.mjs`, `e1-score.mjs`, and `e1-stats.mjs` (ADR 011) are imported by
`e1-scan-run.mjs`, `e1-fts-run.mjs`, `e1-verify-score.mjs`, and `e1-ladder-score.mjs`
(ADR 012); `harness-embedder.mjs` has 16 importers spanning four tracks.

Worse, two guards classify by scanning `eval/*.mjs` — `eval/results-writers.mjs` (D025) and
`eval/__tests__/median-single-source.test.mjs` (D016/D024). Both **pass silently on an empty
set**. Moving their targets would have turned two working pins green-and-inert, which is the
precise failure mode they exist to prevent.

`EVAL.md` manifests deliver the navigation without touching a single import.

## Consequences

- A reader can answer "why is X the way it is" from a ~200-line document instead of a
  11,459-line one, with the full record one link away.
- Numeric order is **not** reading order: ADR 001 is the scheme itself, and 002–013 are
  ordered by decision date, so the empirical method (010) that governs every experiment is
  recorded after the experiments it governs. `adr/README.md` gives a reading order.
- Backfilled ADRs are summaries written after the fact. Where an ADR and its
  `PLAN-EXCERPT.md` disagree, **the excerpt and the scored artifacts win** — the same
  precedence the constitution already gave the plan.
- The `.history/NNN-YYYY-MM-DD-<name>.md` convention (adopted as D5, ADR 009) is unchanged
  and still holds the two superseded plans. ADRs are a live series; `.history/` is a morgue.

## What this does not claim

This restructure moved no evidence and re-derived no number. Every figure in ADRs 002–013 is
carried over from the plan or from `FINDINGS.md`; none was recomputed from the journals as
part of the split. An ADR asserting a number is a **citation**, not a fresh measurement.
